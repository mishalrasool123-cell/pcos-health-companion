    PCOS Education

    Learn simple health information from reliable sources. This section is for education and does not replace medical advice.

    What is PCOS?

    PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) is a common hormonal condition. It can affect menstrual cycles, hormone levels, and other aspects of health. Having some PCOS-related signs does not mean that a person has PCOS.

    When should you talk to a doctor?

    Consider speaking with a healthcare professional if you have persistent changes in your periods, symptoms that concern you, or questions about your reproductive or hormonal health.

    Important

    Remember: PCOS-related symptoms can have different causes. This app cannot diagnose PCOS. If you are concerned about your symptoms, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.
